Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur is a beautiful city known for its forts, palaces and rich culture. It is also called the Pink City of India.

Famous Places

Some famous places in Jaipur are Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace and Jantar Mantar.

Popular Food

Jaipur is famous for traditional Rajasthani food. Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar and Kachori are some popular foods of the city.
